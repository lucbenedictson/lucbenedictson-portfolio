Featured · iOS · Aug 2024

OnTrack – Success & Wellness

App Store GitHub

A goal tracker for career, health, and fitness. It grew out of my Swift Student Challenge app, and I designed, built, and published it to the App Store on my own.

The week calendar
Swipe between weeks to pick a day. Dots mark days that have tasks, and they respect whatever category and completion filters you have on. The selected day stays highlighted. I started from a Stack Overflow example and built these details on top.
Remembers how you left it
Tasks, filters, sort order, and the selected date are saved with Codable and UserDefaults, so the app reopens exactly where you were.
Built with
SwiftUI and MVVM, with shared view models for tasks and preferences.
